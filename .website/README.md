Unicorns. They're so hot right now.

Everyone wants to know what they're up to, what they are doing, when they're eating,  what color they are, how many 'corns they have!

We pay a pretty penny for the right picts.

Watch out for the dark ones though. We don't want pics of those. Not colorful enough, you see.

=========

Desktop/Laptop: 
Mouse capture (click the screen)
+/- for zoom (or use trackpad two fingers)
Space bar to snap a picture.

Mobile:
Uses phone IMU to look around. 
Zoom with two fingers. 
Tap anywhere to take a picture.
